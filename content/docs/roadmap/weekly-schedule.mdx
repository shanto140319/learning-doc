---
title: "Weekly schedule"
description: "How 13 hours a week splits across study, English, building and job hunting."
---

The plan assumes 13 hours a week alongside your job: short weekday sessions plus one long weekend block. Consistency beats intensity; a missed day is fine, a missed week is not.

| Slot | Hours | What happens |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Sun–Thu mornings or evenings, 60 min | 5 | Core technical study for the current phase |
| Daily, 15–20 min (commute or lunch) | 2 | English: listening, shadowing, vocabulary |
| Two weekday evenings, 45 min | 1.5 | English tutor sessions (from month 1) |
| Friday, 4 hours | 4 | Project building: the deep-work block |
| Saturday, 30 min | 0.5 | DSA: 2 problems in TypeScript |
| Saturday, 30 min (from month 7: 2–3 hrs) | 0.5 | Job hunt: applications, LinkedIn, networking |

From month 7 the split shifts: study drops to 3 hours and job hunting rises to 3 hours a week. Use AI assistants for every study session: ask them to quiz you, review your code and explain errors, but write the code yourself first.
