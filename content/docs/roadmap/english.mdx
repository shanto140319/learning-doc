---
title: "English track"
description: "Building interview-level spoken English over 12 months."
---

Goal: speak clearly and calmly for 60 minutes in a technical interview, and follow fast native speakers in meetings. You don't need a perfect accent; you need clarity, structure and confidence.

## Stage 1 (months 1–3): Fluency habit

- 2 tutor sessions a week (45–50 min) on [Preply](https://preply.com/en/courses/english/business-english-course), where lessons start from a few dollars. Pick a tutor who specialises in business English or IT and interview preparation; budget roughly $10–20 per lesson for a good one
- Daily 15 min shadowing: play a tech talk or podcast, pause, repeat sentences aloud copying rhythm and stress
- Daily 5 min: record yourself explaining what you built today, then listen back once

## Stage 2 (months 4–6): Technical speaking

- Tutor sessions shift to explaining your projects, code reviews and architecture decisions
- Learn "connector" phrases that buy thinking time: "Let me think about that for a second", "The trade-off here is…", "Could you clarify what you mean by…"
- Watch conference talks at 1.25x speed with English subtitles, then without

## Stage 3 (months 7–12): Interview English

- Tutor runs full mock behavioural interviews using your STAR stories
- Practise thinking aloud while solving DSA problems: this is the most common weakness for non-native candidates
- Join one English-speaking developer community call or meetup a month (online is fine)

Tip: use an AI voice assistant for extra free speaking practice between tutor sessions, and ask it to point out repeated grammar mistakes.
