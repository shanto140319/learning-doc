The job hunt is a funnel: most applications get no reply, so volume and quality both matter. Start building your profile in month 1 even though applications begin in month 7.

## Your public profile (set up in month 1, polish every month)

1. LinkedIn headline: "Full-Stack Engineer · React/Next.js · NestJS · AI products". Set location to Bangladesh and "Open to remote". Post one short build update every two weeks.
2. GitHub: pin your 2 projects and the open-source PR; every repo has a README with screenshots and a live link.
3. A one-page CV in English: impact in numbers ("cut page load from 4s to 1.2s"), not task lists.
4. A simple personal site on your own domain linking everything together.

## Where to apply (in order of priority)

| Channel | Why | Weekly effort |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Vetted talent platforms: Arc, Turing, Toptal | They do the matching; one vetting process leads to many offers | Pass vetting once, then respond to matches |
| Remote job boards: Wellfound, We Work Remotely, Remote OK, Himalayas | Startups that hire globally | 6–8 applications |
| LinkedIn jobs filtered to "Remote" + direct messages to hiring managers | Direct contact often beats the portal | 4–5 applications + 5 messages |
| Referrals from Bangladeshi engineers already working remotely | Highest response rate | Reach out to 2 people a week |
| Upwork long-term contracts (bridge only) | Income and USD client references while you hunt | Optional |

## Funnel targets for months 7–12

- 150+ applications → aim for 15–25 first calls → 6–10 technical rounds → 1–3 offers
- If you get fewer than 1 reply per 15 applications, fix your CV and LinkedIn before sending more

## Getting paid in Bangladesh

Most remote employers pay through an Employer of Record or contractor platform (Deel, Remote, Plane and similar), Payoneer, or a direct bank wire. Payoneer payments can be withdrawn to a Bangladeshi bank or [straight to bKash](https://unb.com.bd/category/Bangladesh/bkash-to-bring-payments-to-freelancers-through-payoneer-in-real-time/87424). Before signing, confirm the payment method, currency, invoice schedule and who covers transfer fees, and check current tax and remittance rules with a local tax adviser.

For reference, salary data from the payroll platform [Plane](https://plane.com/salaries/software-developer/bangladesh) puts the median remote software developer in Bangladesh at about $46,000 a year, consistent with the target range in section 1.
