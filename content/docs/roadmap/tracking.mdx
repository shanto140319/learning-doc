Review this plan on the last Friday of every month. Score yourself honestly; two red months in a row means the plan needs adjusting, not abandoning.

## Monthly scorecard

| Measure | Target |
| --- | --- |
| Study + build hours logged | 50+ per month |
| Something shipped publicly (feature, post, PR) | At least 2 |
| Tutor sessions completed | 8 |
| DSA problems | 8–10 |
| Applications sent (from month 7) | 40–60 |

## Risks and how to handle them

- **Too many side projects.** The mobile apps, the small business and new ideas compete for the same 13 hours. Park them until the remote offer is signed; your two portfolio projects are the only side projects, because they double as interview material.
- **Course collecting.** If you have watched more than you built in a month, stop buying and build.
- **Fear of speaking.** Book the next tutor session before the current one ends so skipping takes effort.
- **Rejections in months 7–9.** Normal. Treat each one as data: log the question you missed and fix it.
- **Burnout.** Keep one full day off every week; a sustainable 13 hours beats a 30-hour week followed by a month off.
