Goal: a signed remote offer by October 2027. Interviews are a skill; most of this phase is repetition until each round type feels routine.

## Months 7–8 — Interview preparation

- System design: URL shortener, chat app, file storage, notification system, rate limiter. Study from *System Design Interview* by Alex Xu (Vol. 1) and practise drawing them out loud
- Frontend system design: designing a news feed, an autocomplete, a design system; performance and rendering strategies in Next.js
- Behavioural: write 8 STAR stories from your 5 years (a conflict, a failure, an ownership moment, a hard deadline, mentoring, a technical decision you'd change)
- Take-home practice: build two small take-homes in 4 hours each, under time pressure, each with a clear README, since remote teams judge written communication. Also practise live-coding rounds (build a small feature in 60–90 minutes while talking) and code-review rounds (find the problems in someone else's pull request)
- DSA: reach 100 problems; focus on medium-level patterns rather than volume

## Months 7–12 — Applications (runs in parallel)

- Apply to 10–15 roles a week, each with a tailored CV summary and a short personal note
- Book 2 paid mock interviews a month (one technical, one system design) with senior engineers
- After every real interview, write down every question within an hour and fix the weak answers

## Months 9–12 — Close

- Negotiate: always ask for the range first; anchor in USD; get the contract type (full-time contractor, EOR) in writing
- Keep your current job until the first remote payment arrives in your account

## Phase 3 deliverables

- [ ] 8 STAR stories written and rehearsed aloud
- [ ] 5 system designs drawn and explained on video to yourself
- [ ] 100 DSA problems in TypeScript
- [ ] 150+ tailored applications sent
- [ ] Remote offer signed
